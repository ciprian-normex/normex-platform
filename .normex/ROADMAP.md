# NORMEX Plant MVP Roadmap

## P1 — Asset master control

- Finish Asset Edit
- Master asset data and clear immutable fields

## P2 — Location & Deployment

- Confirm current location, project assignment, release/reassignment and history

## P3 — Movements

- From/to, dates, transport, status, completion and history

## P4 — Defects & Repairs

- Report, assess restrictions/safe-to-use, repair progress, downtime, authorised cost, return to service and close

## P5 — Maintenance

- Planned maintenance, due/overdue position, service record, completion and evidence/history

## P6 — Compliance

- Requirement matrix, certificates/evidence, expiry, holds and upload/revision once Storage permits

## P7 — Requests

- Request, review, approve/reject, source/allocate, fulfil and close

## P8 — Availability

- Present/future availability, commitments, demand, clashes, gaps, idle Plant and unnecessary hire exposure

## P9 — Suppliers

- Supplier register, hire relationship, operational contacts and authorised performance/commercial context

## P10 — Plant integration pass

- Overview drill-downs, Fleet filters, navigation, duplicated UI, empty/loading/error states and mobile issues

## P11 — Plant MVP hardening

After the workflows are substantially built: deeper targeted tests, permission and tenant review, write/transition and calculation review, browser compatibility and final UX polish.
